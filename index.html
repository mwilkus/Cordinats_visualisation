Tips
  • Non-orthogonal systems are fine – e.g. x = u + v*cos(alpha), y = v*sin(alpha).
  • Ranges may use parameters, e.g. min = a+0.01, and may be unbounded: inf, -inf.
  • Mark angle-like coordinates as periodic (⟳) so sliders wrap.
  • The metric, its inverse and Christoffel symbols are derived symbolically from your formulas.
